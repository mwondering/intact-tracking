# Provenance and Attribution

The learning architecture follows the method described in **INTACT: Isomorphic
Intent-to-Action Learning for Search-Free World Models** by Junhan Sun, Hao
Zhao, and Guofeng Zhang.

This distribution contains the MJLab task configuration, policy modules, and
G1 model assets required to reconstruct its supported rollout checkpoint. The
MJLab, MuJoCo, RSL-RL, PyTorch, and TensorDict packages are installed as normal
Python dependencies and remain governed by their own licenses.

Motion datasets and policy checkpoints are user-supplied runtime inputs and are
not distributed by this repository.
